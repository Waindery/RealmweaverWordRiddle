 THE WORD ODYSSEY

Game Genre:Educational Puzzle Game

2. Gameplay Mechanics (Core Mechanics)
The gameplay revolves around solving word and grammar puzzles tied to the 
mythos of Homeros's epic across 5 levels.
Multiple grammar and vocabulary questions 
Drag and drop sentence building 
Matching words with definitions
Interactive short story challenges
3. Game Concept and Lore
3.1. Core Concept
A puzzle game where players test their vocabulary and grammar skills by 
navigating a theme based on Ancient Greek mythology, ultimately solving the 
mysteries of Homeros’s The Odyssey.
3.2. Story (Lore)
The player is a curious child wearing a mysterious antique key around his neck. 
He found the key near the secluded Temple of Athena deep in the woods. His 
goal is to enter the Temple and discover the key's purpose. The Temple holds a 
great treasure: The Odyssey by Homeros, the key to wisdom and knowledge. The 
key is the only object that can unlock this literary treasure.
3.3. Themes & Emotional Goals
• Empowerment through knowledge
• Curiosity and discovery
• The joy of solving problems with words
4. Theme and Atmosphere
Theme: Ancient Greek Mythology and the Epic of The Odyssey.
Visual Atmosphere: A warm and detailed pixel art aesthetic created with 
Aseprite. The temple interior is mysterious and ancient, while the levels (Trojan 
War, polyphemus,sea voyages, calypso’s island, return to ithaca) feature iconic 
mythological imagery suitable for their themes.
5. Character Designs
Player (Child): A small, curious child rendered in pixel art. Wears the Antique 
Key around their neck, which will unlock the final treasure chest in Level 5.
NPCs:
1.Hermes(Mentor): Appears on the screen immediately after the Main Menu. 
Directs the player, explaining that the Temple belongs to Athena and holds a 
treasure. He guides the player toward Level 5.
2. Calypso(Obstacle): Appears after Level 3. Attempts to convince the player to 
go to Level 4, preventing them from accessing the ultimate treasure.
6. Level Design
The levels were designed according to the chronological order of the Odyssey 
epic.
Level 1: Trojan War. Finding 5 Themed Words. The words are; trojan horse, 
golden apple, grape, shield, ship, fire.
Level 2: Odysseus and Polyphemus. Finding 5 Themed Words.
Level 3: Odysseus and Poseidon. Finding 5 Themed Words.
Split Path: Calypso's Persuasion. Calypso tries to persuade player to go to 4. 
Level.
Level 4: Calypso's Island. Finding 5 Themed Words. After finding the words, the 
game ends with no reward.
Level 5: Return to Ithaca. Player finds the treasure.
7. Sound and Music Design
7.1. Background Music: The musical style will be Ancient Orchestral, 
characterized by a calm and flowing pace that maintains atmospheric depth 
without disrupting the focus or the educational rhythm of the gameplay.
7.2. SFX: We will add button click sounds, level compilation sounds.
